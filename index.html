01 · Research vision

Privacy that can still be trusted.

My work sits at the intersection of theoretical cryptography and privacy-preserving systems, with particular attention to anonymous credentials, zero-knowledge proofs, verifiable computation, and provable security.

“How can a system protect a person’s privacy while remaining verifiable, accountable, and practical?”
Doctoral thesis Privacy with Accountability: Verifiable and Modular Cryptographic Systems
Institution School of Informatics, University of Edinburgh
Supervisors Prof. Markulf Kohlweiss and Prof. Anna Lysyanskaya
01

Privacy with accountability

Systems that protect users while retaining meaningful guarantees against misuse, framing, or unverifiable behavior.

02

Anonymous credentials

Credential mechanisms that let people prove relevant attributes without revealing unnecessary identity information.

03

Verifiable computation

Methods for computing over protected data while enabling efficient verification of the result.

04

Provable security

Formal models, reductions, and security arguments that make protocol guarantees precise and defensible.
